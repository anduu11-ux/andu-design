Cookie Policy

This website does not use cookies. Here's what that means and what would change it.

Last updated 24 September 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser so it can recognise you later. Similar technologies work the same way: local and session storage, tracking pixels and browser fingerprinting.

What this site uses

None of them. This site:

  • sets no cookies, first-party or third-party;
  • stores nothing in your browser's local or session storage;
  • uses no analytics, advertising or social-media tools;
  • embeds no third-party content, such as videos, maps or social widgets, that could set cookies of its own.

That is why there is no cookie banner. Consent is only needed for cookies that aren't strictly necessary, and there are none.

[Check with your hosting provider that it adds no cookies of its own. Some CDNs set security cookies, for example for bot protection. If it does, list each one here with its name, purpose and lifetime. Strictly necessary security cookies don't need consent.]

Your browser's cache

Like on any website, your browser keeps copies of files such as images, fonts and code so pages load faster next time. That's ordinary caching. It holds nothing that identifies you, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Other websites

Our project pages link to sites we built, such as mc-fiduciaire.be. Once you follow a link, that site's own cookie policy applies.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or embedded content that uses cookies, we'll list every cookie here and ask for your consent before setting any cookie that isn't strictly necessary.
