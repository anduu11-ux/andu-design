## Design Specificity Verdict

**Design review:** The content is specific, but the frame around it isn't. Near-black with a violet-to-cyan glow, Geist, a pill above a two-tone headline, glass-framed screenshots, aurora and grain: that's the standard developer-tool or AI launch look, and with a different headline it could sell a database.
- **The terminal intro is the clearest mismatch.** `$ npx create-website your-business` means nothing to an accountant, and a one-command "build" makes a website sound like a commodity, the opposite of "crafted".
- **The proof is what grounds the page:** real screenshots, 500+ clients, seven named languages. Ironically, the client's warm, photo-led site in those screenshots would persuade a business owner better than the faceless page around it.

The terminal story and the aurora were the user's picks from recommended options, so this is a question of intent, not a defect.

**Detector:** The command-line scan found 14 issues, but v2.html and all three JSX files came back clean. The in-browser scan found 2 on the home page, 11 on Privacy and 4 on Cookies.
- **Agrees with the design review:** `radial-halo` fires on every page. It's the violet glow on a dark page, the same "stock launch look" signal.
- **Caught something the review missed:** `line-length`, 10 times on the legal pages. Lines run 90–99 characters, where comfortable reading is 45–75.
- **False positives:**
  - `bounce-easing` is tripped by the name `cue-bounce`; the animation is a 6px ease-in-out with no overshoot.
  - `buried-raster` on the aurora: the hidden browser pane never ran its scroll reveal.
  - `repeating-stripes-gradient` is the intro's segmented progress bar.
  - `flat-type-hierarchy` on the legal pages: the static scan can't read the CSS rules nested inside `.legal`, and the browser measured the headings at 56px and 22px.
  - The `cramped-padding`, radius and colour hits all come from the temporary yellow placeholders.
- **Documentation drift, not UI bugs:** DESIGN.md says "Geist" while the CSS loads "Geist Variable", and the smallest sizes of the fluid headings (34–42px) aren't in its type scale.
- **Not checked:** pixel-level contrast, because the hidden pane never drew a frame. The dim headline over the contour lines is unverified.

**Overlays:** The detector ran inside all three pages, but in the assessment's own tab, which was then closed. No overlay is left.

## Overall Impression
It's beautifully made and aimed at the wrong person. The craft is well above typical: one left edge, one accent, the contour field, careful accessibility. But the page speaks to developers, while the buyer is a business owner deciding whether to email a stranger. The biggest opportunity is to make contacting the studio work, then move the persuasion from the shell (terminal, glow) to the proof and the people.

## What's Working

- **One system, held tightly.** Nav, headline, sections and footer share one left edge. There's one accent, and hierarchy comes from white at different strengths.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The only way to contact the studio can't work yet**
- **Why it matters:** the page has one job, and the address is still `hello@example.com`. Even with a real address, it lives only inside a mailto link. People on webmail, or on a PC with no mail app set up, click "Get in touch" and nothing useful happens, and there's no address on screen to copy.
- **Fix:** show the real address as text with a copy button next to the email button. Pre-fill the email's subject and a few prompts (your business, what you need, which languages).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The intro is written for developers and taxes every visit**
- **Why it matters:**
  - For 4.6 seconds an accountant watches `npx` and fake loading.
  - It replays on every load, including when someone clicks "Projects" on the Privacy page.
  - Scrolling with the mouse wheel during it moves the page underneath, so the visitor lands on a blank stretch when it ends.
- **Fix:**
  - Rewrite it in plain language, or cut it.
  - Play it only on a fresh arrival: skip it when the URL has a `#section` or the visitor came from another page of the site. That needs no storage, so the cookie policy stays true.
  - Treat wheel and touch scrolling as a skip.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

**[P1] "Proof, not promises" shows features, not outcomes, and the studio has no people**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The page praises the client's team page "so clients know who they're talking to", yet shows no names or faces for the studio.
  - "First" appears three times (pill, label, lead), which reads as "only one client".
  - The closing call to action is the highest-stakes moment, and it has no face, location, price range or reply time.
- **Fix:** add these, all from the studio's real details:
  - a short attributed quote from MC Fiduciaire
  - one outcome number, if there is one
  - a strip with the founders' names, faces and the languages they speak
  - one reassurance line by the button, for example "we reply within one working day"
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The hierarchy works against the message**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The payoff, "grow your business", is the dimmest text on the page (40% white). On phones the dark overlay is a flat 72%, so the contour lines run straight through it.
  - The nav's white "Contact" button competes with "See our work"; DESIGN.md itself says one white button per view.
  - At 375px the closing headline breaks as "Let's build your / website.", because it's the only two-tone headline whose dim half doesn't start its own line.
  - The section title (64px) and the project name (56px) are nearly the same size.
- **Fix:** brighten the benefit line, make the nav Contact an outline or text link, put the closing headline's dim half on its own line like the others, and bring the project name down to about 44px.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] Motion and small-screen edge cases**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The contour canvas keeps moving when a visitor asks for reduced motion, against the site's own rule; the rest of the page honours it.
  - The 150svh hero leaves about a third of a screen of scrolling where nothing is readable, which can look like the end of the page.
  - On a phone held sideways, the hero buttons start below the fold and only appear once the hero is already fading.
- **Fix:** draw one still frame of the canvas under reduced motion, shorten the hero to about 115svh, and only pin and fade the hero on screens at least 600px tall.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable animate`
